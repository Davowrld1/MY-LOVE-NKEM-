My Love(NKEM) ❤️💕.
I don’t know if words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me, but I want you to know that you are one of the most beautiful blessings life has given me. You are not just someone I love; you are the woman I see in my future, the one I want to build a home with, grow old with, laugh with, pray with, and share every chapter of my life with. Honestly, I don’t just see you as my girlfriend anymore; in my heart, I already see you as my wife. ❤️ I want to be there for you in your happiest moments and stand beside you through your difficult days. I want to celebrate your victories, encourage your dreams, wipe away your worries, and remind you every day that you are loved, valued, respected and appreciated. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect, but I can promise to keep choosing you, to keep learning how to love you better, and to never take what we share for granted. My biggest wish is that years from now, we’ll look back at where we started, smile at how far we’ve come, and be grateful that we never gave up on each other. I don’t want a temporary love with you. I want a lifetime. I want the ordinary mornings, the little arguments we eventually laugh about, the beautiful memories, the family we may build, and all the adventures life has waiting for us. If I had to choose again, in a thousand different lifetimes, my heart would still find its way to you. You are my today, my tomorrow, and the woman I hope to call my wife for the rest of my life. I love you more than words can ever fully express. ❤️💍
